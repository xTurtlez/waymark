4Waymark brand · four logo directions

Waymark is bought by law firms and used by their clients, so the mark has to hold up next to a firm's own logo, read as serious software in a procurement deck, and still feel gentle on a bereaved family's phone. Each direction is shown large, as a lockup on light and dark, as an app icon and at favicon size. My pick is 4c: the mark and the product's progress path become the same thing.
